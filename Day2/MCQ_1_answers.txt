Section 1: Deep Learning Overview & Core Architectures
======================================================================

Q1. What is Deep Learning?
A) A rule-based programming approach using explicit decision trees
B) A subset of Machine Learning focusing on neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns in data
C) A database query technique for structured tabular data only
D) A hardware acceleration standard for CPUs
Answer: B) A subset of Machine Learning focusing on neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns in data

Q2. Which list includes all five parts of Deep Learning explicitly named in the notes?
A) ANN, CNN, RNN, LSTM, Transformers
B) SVM, CNN, k-NN, LSTM, Naive Bayes
C) ANN, PCA, RNN, Random Forest, Transformers
D) CNN, RNN, DBSCAN, Linear Regression, Transformers
Answer: A) ANN, CNN, RNN, LSTM, Transformers

Q3. How is an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) defined in the provided notes?
A) A statistical clustering algorithm that only operates on labeled spreadsheets
B) A computational model emulating biological neural networks in the human brain using interconnected nodes ("neurons")
C) A rule engine that requires explicit step-by-step instructions for every prediction
D) A compression algorithm used solely for image files
Answer: B) A computational model emulating biological neural networks in the human brain using interconnected nodes ("neurons")

Q4. Which architecture is specialized for grid-like data such as images by learning spatial hierarchies of features?
A) LSTM
B) CNN
C) Transformer
D) Single-layer Perceptron
Answer: B) CNN

Q5. Which architecture uses self-attention mechanisms to process sequences in parallel?
A) CNN
B) RNN
C) Transformer
D) McCulloch-Pitts neuron
Answer: C) Transformer

Q6. How do LSTMs improve upon standard RNNs according to the notes?
A) They remove all hidden layers from sequence models
B) They capture long-term dependencies in sequences and address the vanishing gradient problem
C) They convert sequential text into static 2D images
D) They train without using any data
Answer: B) They capture long-term dependencies in sequences and address the vanishing gradient problem

Section 2: History & Modern Computing Drivers
======================================================================

Q7. Who introduced artificial neurons in 1943?
A) Frank Rosenblatt
B) Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert
C) Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts
D) Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun
Answer: C) Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts

Q8. What occurred in 1969 according to the timeline?
A) Frank Rosenblatt introduced the perceptron
B) Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert highlighted limitations of the perceptron
C) Backpropagation became a popular training method
D) Transformers replaced all neural networks
Answer: B) Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert highlighted limitations of the perceptron

Q9. Which decade saw the backpropagation algorithm become a popular method for training neural networks in the notes?
A) 1940s
B) 1950s
C) 1980s
D) 2010s
Answer: C) 1980s

Q10. Which combination drove the prominence of Deep Learning starting in the late 2000s?
A) Smaller datasets and single-core processors
B) Powerful GPUs, large datasets, and the realization that deeper neural networks can achieve remarkable results on complex tasks
C) Rule-based expert systems and punch cards
D) Eliminating hidden layers from neural networks
Answer: B) Powerful GPUs, large datasets, and the realization that deeper neural networks can achieve remarkable results on complex tasks
